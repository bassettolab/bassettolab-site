I live in the state of São Paulo, Brazil. I like music, books, and technology — which is already enough to fill more hours than there are in a day. I work with Oracle Cloud, mostly trying to understand why systems that are supposed to make life simpler sometimes do exactly the opposite.

Between one query and another configuration, I like studying different subjects, writing down my own conclusions, and keeping track of what I learn along the way.

This blog exists for exactly that reason: to work as a kind of backup for my brain, because after 40 it starts showing a few performance issues, data loss, and the occasional timeout.

In the end, I'm interested in a lot of things and absolutely specialized in none of them — which, depending on how you look at it, is either a problem or a long-term strategy.
